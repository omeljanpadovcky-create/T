PRIVATE SESSION · SCREEN SHARING

Share your screen by choice.

This page is a transparent starter interface. It does not capture or transmit your screen.

Before sharing
  • Confirm who will be able to view your screen.
  • Close private apps and notifications you do not want to share.
  • On iPhone, use a supported screen-sharing app and approve Apple's system broadcast prompt.

Full-device iPhone sharing requires a compatible native iOS implementation and a secure streaming backend. Safari cannot silently start whole-screen capture. The sharer must start and be able to stop the broadcast.

No screen data is collected by this demo.
